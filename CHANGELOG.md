# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release, for Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric, NeoForge and Forge.

- Adds Unbreaking IV and Efficiency VI as enchanted books.
- Librarians offer them at the novice level for 30 to 64 emeralds (40 on average) plus a book.
- Every bastion remnant, ancient city and abandoned mineshaft has at least one of them in one of its chests. Two or three are possible but rare.
- End city chests can hold one, and fishing can catch one, both with a very low chance.
- Two Efficiency V give Efficiency VI and two Unbreaking III give Unbreaking IV in an anvil, with books or tools.
- The enchanting table, loot tables, and the random book a librarian sells never go above Unbreaking III or Efficiency V.
- Everything is adjustable in `config/effirromplus.json`.
- Server side only: players join with plain Minecraft.
